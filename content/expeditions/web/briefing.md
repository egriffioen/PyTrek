title: A note from the ranger station
signoff: Ruth

Welcome to Pythonia. I'm glad you're here, and I'm sorry your first week
won't be a quiet one.

Thirty-one years I've walked these trails, and in all that time the one thing
hikers could count on was TrailNet, our park website. Every trail, every
distance, every "turn left at the big rock." Volunteers built it page by page.

Three weeks ago it started going wrong. A trail page that used to say *easy*
now says *strenuous*. The Juniper Pass page is just... gone. Signposts on the
site point to places that don't exist. Last Saturday a family followed
TrailNet's directions to Hidden Falls and ended up in the maintenance yard.
They were very polite about it. The goats were not.

Nobody on staff changed those pages. I checked the edit logs myself, and
whoever did this left no name, just changes, made at odd hours, faster than
any person could type.

I can read a trail, but I can't read code. You can. I need someone who can
ask the website questions and understand its answers: which pages are gone,
what's been changed, and who has been crawling around in there.

Start small. Learn how the site talks. The clues will add up.

I've left a few of the hiker reviews on your desk. Read them. That's who we're
doing this for.
